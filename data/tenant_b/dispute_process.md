# Horizon Bank — Transaction Dispute Process

If a Horizon Bank customer does not recognise a transaction on their
account:

1. They should first check the merchant name against recent purchases,
   subscriptions, or family-member spending.
2. If still unrecognised, lodge a dispute within 60 days of the transaction
   date via online banking or by visiting a branch.
3. Horizon Bank will provisionally credit the disputed amount within 10
   business days while the investigation is open, for amounts under
   $2,000.
4. Investigations are typically resolved within 21 days, and up to 120 days
   for transactions involving an overseas merchant.

Horizon Bank charges a $0 fee for the first dispute lodged each year, and
a $15 administration fee for subsequent disputes found to be customer
error.
