# Globex Insurance — Policy Terms

Tenant: Globex Insurance (tenant_b)

## Products
- **Globex Home & Contents** — combined building and contents cover.
- **Globex Motor** — comprehensive and third-party options.
- **Globex Landlord** — cover for rental properties.

## Premium factors
- Location and risk rating of the insured address
- Claims history over the past 5 years
- Sum insured and chosen excess

## Cooling-off period
You have 21 days from the policy start date to cancel for a full refund, provided no
claim has been made.

## Renewal
Policies renew annually. Globex sends a renewal notice at least 14 days before expiry.
