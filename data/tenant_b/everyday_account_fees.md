# Horizon Bank — Everyday Account Fee Schedule

The Horizon Everyday transaction account is Horizon Bank's primary
day-to-day banking product. The following fees apply unless a fee waiver
condition below is met.

## Standard fees

- Monthly account-keeping fee: $0 — Horizon Bank does not charge an
  account-keeping fee on the Everyday account, regardless of balance or
  age.
- ATM withdrawal at a Horizon Bank ATM: free, unlimited.
- ATM withdrawal at a non-Horizon Bank ATM within Australia: free, up to
  5 withdrawals per month, then $2.00 per withdrawal thereafter.
- ATM withdrawal overseas: $4.00 per withdrawal, no foreign transaction
  fee.
- Dishonour fee (insufficient funds for a scheduled payment): $12, capped
  at three dishonour fees per account per month.
- Overdrawn account fee: $0 — Horizon Bank does not charge an overdrawn
  account fee, but unarranged overdrawn balances accrue interest under the
  overdraft facility terms below.
- Paper statement fee: $5 per statement; electronic statements are free.
- Replacement card fee: free, unlimited replacements.

## Fee waivers

There is no linked-product waiver scheme on the Horizon Everyday account,
since the account-keeping fee is already $0 for all customers.

## Overdraft facility

An approved overdraft on a Horizon Everyday account accrues interest at
13.49% p.a., calculated daily on the overdrawn balance and charged
monthly. Overdraft limits range from $500 to $10,000 and are subject to a
separate credit assessment.
