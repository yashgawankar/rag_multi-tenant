# Horizon Bank — International Transfers and Foreign Currency Fees

## Outbound international transfers

Sending money overseas through Horizon Online Banking or a Horizon Bank
branch attracts the following fees, in addition to any margin built into
the exchange rate applied at the time of the transfer:

- Online transfer (Horizon Bank mobile app or internet banking): $15 flat
  fee.
- Branch-initiated transfer: $30 flat fee.
- Urgent/same-day transfer: not offered by Horizon Bank; all international
  transfers are processed within standard timeframes.
- Receiving bank and intermediary bank fees are outside Horizon Bank's
  control and may be deducted from the amount received by the beneficiary.

## Inbound international transfers

Horizon Bank does not charge a fee to receive an international transfer
into a Horizon transaction or savings account. The exchange rate applied
is Horizon Bank's standard buy rate at the time funds are received,
published daily on the Horizon Bank website.

## Foreign currency card transactions

Purchases made overseas, or online purchases billed in a foreign currency,
incur no foreign transaction fee on either the Horizon debit card or the
Horizon Plus credit card (see the Horizon Plus Credit Card document for
full credit card terms).

## Daily and monthly limits

- Online international transfers: $15,000 per transaction, $25,000 per
  rolling 24-hour period.
- Branch-initiated transfers: up to $500,000 per transaction, subject to
  identity verification and source-of-funds checks for amounts over
  $100,000, in line with Horizon Bank's anti-money-laundering obligations.

## Transfer cancellation

An international transfer can be cancelled free of charge if it has not
yet been processed by the receiving bank. Once processed, cancellation is
not guaranteed and may incur a $30 recall fee charged by the receiving
bank, deducted at Horizon Bank's discretion from the customer's account.
