# Horizon Bank — Home Loan Product Disclosure Statement

This document summarises the key features of the Horizon Standard Variable
Home Loan and the Horizon Fixed Home Loan. It is not a substitute for the
full Product Disclosure Statement.

## Defined terms

"Comparison Rate" means the rate calculated on a $150,000 loan over a
25-year term, including fees, for comparison purposes only. "LVR" means
Loan to Value Ratio, the loan amount expressed as a percentage of the
lender's valuation of the security property.

## Horizon Standard Variable Home Loan

- Interest rate: 6.49% p.a. variable (comparison rate 6.58% p.a.).
- Minimum loan amount: $20,000. Maximum LVR: 95% (Lenders Mortgage
  Insurance applies above 80% LVR).
- Offset account: available for a $10/month fee, reduces interest
  calculated on the loan by the offset balance.
- Extra repayments: unlimited, with no early repayment fee.
- Redraw: available on extra repayments, minimum redraw amount $200.

## Horizon Fixed Home Loan

- Interest rate: 6.10% p.a. fixed for terms of 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 years
  (comparison rate 6.62% p.a. for the 3-year term).
- Extra repayments: limited to $20,000 per annum during the fixed period;
  amounts above this incur a break cost calculated under clause 11 of the
  full PDS.
- At the end of the fixed term, the loan reverts to the Horizon Standard
  Variable Home Loan rate then current, unless the customer re-fixes.

## Fees

- Application fee: $250 flat, for owner-occupier and investment loans alike.
- Annual package fee: $0 — Horizon Bank does not charge a package fee.
- Discharge fee: $250, payable on full repayment or refinance to another
  lender.
- Late payment fee: $20, charged once per missed repayment, not
  compounding.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be 18 years or older, an Australian citizen, permanent
resident, or eligible visa holder, and meet Horizon Bank's standard
responsible lending serviceability assessment, which includes a minimum
2% buffer above the applicable interest rate when assessing repayment
capacity.
