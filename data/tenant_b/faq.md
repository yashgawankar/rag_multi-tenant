# Globex Insurance — Frequently Asked Questions

Tenant: Globex Insurance (tenant_b)

## How do I update my insured address?
Sign in to the Globex portal → My Policies → Update details. Premium may be recalculated.

## Can I pay my premium monthly?
Yes. Monthly instalments are available at no extra charge for annual policies paid by
direct debit.

## What is the excess on a windscreen claim?
Windscreen-only claims on Globex Motor have a reduced excess of $100.

## How do I add a driver to my motor policy?
Go to My Policies → Motor → Drivers → Add driver. Drivers under 25 may increase your
premium.

## How long does a claim assessment take?
Standard claims are assessed within 10 business days. You can track status in the portal.
