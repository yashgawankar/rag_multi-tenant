# Globex Insurance — Claims Policy

Tenant: Globex Insurance (tenant_b)

## Overview
Globex Insurance handles home, contents, and motor claims. This document describes
how claims are lodged and assessed.

## Claim timeframes
- Acknowledgement: within 1 business day of lodgement
- Assessment decision: within 10 business days for standard claims
- Catastrophe / event claims: priority handling, up to 20 business days

## Excess
- Standard motor excess: $750
- Young driver excess (under 25): additional $400
- Home contents excess: $500

## How to lodge a claim
1. Sign in to the Globex portal.
2. Select "Lodge a claim" and choose the policy.
3. Upload photos and a description of the loss or damage.

## What is not covered
- Wear and tear and gradual deterioration
- Damage from unapproved modifications
- Events excluded in the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS)
