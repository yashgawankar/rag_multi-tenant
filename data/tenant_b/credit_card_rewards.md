# Tenant B — Horizon Plus Credit Card 
The Horizon Plus card earns 1.5 points per $1 spent on any transaction,
domestic or international, with no foreign transaction fee.

- Annual fee: $249, never waived.
- Purchase interest rate: 21.49% p.a.
- Interest-free period: up to 45 days on purchases if the balance is paid in
  full each statement period.
- Points expire 24 months after they are earned.
- Lost or stolen card: customers must call the 24/7 Tenant B card support
  line immediately; a replacement card is issued within 2 business days
  (express courier).

## Redeeming points

Points can be redeemed for:

- Cash back directly to the linked transaction account (200 points = $1).
- Merchandise from the Tenant B rewards catalogue.
- Donations to a list of partner charities, matched 10% by Tenant B.
