# Horizon Bank — Privacy and Data Handling Policy Summary

This is a summary of how Horizon Bank collects, uses, and stores personal
information. It does not replace the full Privacy Policy available on the
Horizon Bank website.

## What we collect

Horizon Bank collects identity information (name, date of birth, address,
government-issued ID details), financial information (income, account
balances, transaction history), and behavioural information (login times,
device identifiers, app usage patterns) for the purposes of providing
banking services, meeting know-your-customer obligations, and detecting
fraud.

## How long we keep it

Account and transaction records are retained for 7 years after an account
is closed, in line with Horizon Bank's regulatory record-keeping
obligations. Marketing preference data is retained until the customer
opts out or closes all accounts, whichever happens first.

## Sharing with third parties

Horizon Bank does not sell customer data to third parties. Data may be
shared with: credit reporting bodies (for lending applications), fraud
prevention networks, regulators upon lawful request, and service
providers contracted to process payments or host infrastructure, each
bound by a data processing agreement requiring at least the same level of
protection Horizon Bank applies internally.

## Customer data separation

Horizon Bank's systems are architected so that one customer's data is
never accessible to systems or staff serving a different, unrelated
customer outside of the customer's own service relationship, and access
is logged and subject to periodic internal audit.

## Customer rights

Customers may request a copy of the personal information Horizon Bank
holds about them, request correction of inaccurate information, and
request deletion of information no longer required for a legal or
regulatory purpose, by contacting Horizon Bank support.
