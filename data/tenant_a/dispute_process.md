# Skyline Bank — Transaction Dispute Process

If a Skyline Bank customer does not recognise a transaction on their
account:

1. They should first check the merchant name against recent purchases,
   subscriptions, or family-member spending.
2. If still unrecognised, lodge a dispute within 45 days of the transaction
   date via the Skyline Bank mobile app ("Report a transaction") or by
   calling support.
3. Skyline Bank will provisionally credit the disputed amount within 5
   business days while the investigation is open, for amounts under $1,000.
4. Investigations are typically resolved within 30 days, and up to 90 days
   for transactions involving an overseas merchant.

Skyline Bank does not charge a fee for lodging a dispute, regardless of
outcome.
