# Acme Bank — Frequently Asked Questions

Tenant: Acme Bank (tenant_a)

## How do I reset my password?
Open the Acme Bank app, tap "Forgot password", and follow the SMS verification steps.

## What are the daily transfer limits?
- Pay Anyone (existing payee): $25,000 per day
- New payee: $5,000 for the first 24 hours
- BPAY: $50,000 per day

## How do I report a lost or stolen card?
Call 13 ACME (13 22 63) any time, or lock your card instantly in the app under
Cards → Lock card.

## Is Acme Bank covered by the Financial Claims Scheme?
Yes. Deposits up to $250,000 per account holder are guaranteed by the Australian
Government under the Financial Claims Scheme.

## What are your branch hours?
Most Acme branches open 9:30am–4:00pm Monday to Friday, and 9:30am–12:00pm Saturday.
