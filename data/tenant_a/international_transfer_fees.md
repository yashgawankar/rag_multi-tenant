# Skyline Bank — International Transfers and Foreign Currency Fees

## Outbound international transfers

Sending money overseas through Skyline Online Banking or a Skyline Bank
branch attracts the following fees, in addition to any margin built into
the exchange rate applied at the time of the transfer:

- Online transfer (Skyline Bank mobile app or internet banking): $12 flat
  fee.
- Branch-initiated transfer: $32 flat fee.
- Urgent/same-day transfer: additional $20 surcharge on top of the above.
- Receiving bank and intermediary bank fees are outside Skyline Bank's
  control and may be deducted from the amount received by the beneficiary.

## Inbound international transfers

Skyline Bank does not charge a fee to receive an international transfer
into a Skyline transaction or savings account. The exchange rate applied
is Skyline Bank's standard buy rate at the time funds are received,
published daily on the Skyline Bank website.

## Foreign currency card transactions

Purchases made overseas, or online purchases billed in a foreign currency,
incur a foreign transaction fee of 3% of the AUD-converted transaction
amount when made with a Skyline debit card, and are fee-free when made
with the Skyline Rewards credit card (see the Skyline Rewards Credit Card
document for full credit card terms).

## Daily and monthly limits

- Online international transfers: $10,000 per transaction, $20,000 per
  rolling 24-hour period.
- Branch-initiated transfers: up to $250,000 per transaction, subject to
  identity verification and source-of-funds checks for amounts over
  $50,000, in line with Skyline Bank's anti-money-laundering obligations.

## Transfer cancellation

An international transfer can be cancelled free of charge if it has not
yet been processed by the receiving bank. Once processed, cancellation is
not guaranteed and may incur a $25 recall fee charged by the receiving
bank, deducted at Skyline Bank's discretion from the customer's account.
