# Skyline Bank — Home Loan Product Disclosure Statement

This document summarises the key features of the Skyline Variable Home Loan
and the Skyline Fixed Home Loan. It is not a substitute for the full Product
Disclosure Statement.

## Defined terms

"Comparison Rate" means the rate calculated on a $150,000 loan over a
25-year term, including fees, for comparison purposes only. "LVR" means
Loan to Value Ratio, the loan amount expressed as a percentage of the
lender's valuation of the security property.

## Skyline Variable Home Loan

- Interest rate: 6.19% p.a. variable (comparison rate 6.34% p.a.).
- Minimum loan amount: $50,000. Maximum LVR: 90% (Lenders Mortgage
  Insurance applies above 80% LVR).
- Offset account: available at no additional cost, reduces interest
  calculated on the loan by the offset balance.
- Extra repayments: unlimited, with no early repayment fee.
- Redraw: available on extra repayments, minimum redraw amount $500.

## Skyline Fixed Home Loan

- Interest rate: 5.95% p.a. fixed for terms of 1, 2, 3 or 5 years
  (comparison rate 6.41% p.a. for the 3-year term).
- Extra repayments: limited to $10,000 per annum during the fixed period;
  amounts above this incur a break cost calculated under clause 14 of the
  full PDS.
- At the end of the fixed term, the loan reverts to the Skyline Variable
  Home Loan rate then current, unless the customer re-fixes.

## Fees

- Application fee: $0 for owner-occupier loans, $600 for investment loans.
- Annual package fee: $395 p.a., waived in year 1.
- Discharge fee: $350, payable on full repayment or refinance to another
  lender.
- Late payment fee: $15, charged once per missed repayment, not compounding.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be 18 years or older, an Australian citizen or permanent
resident, and meet Skyline Bank's standard responsible lending
serviceability assessment, which includes a minimum 1% buffer above the
applicable interest rate when assessing repayment capacity.
