# Acme Bank — Refund Policy

Tenant: Acme Bank (tenant_a)

## Overview
Acme Bank offers refunds on eligible fees and disputed transactions in line with
the Acme Customer Charter.

## Eligible refunds
- Duplicate transaction charges
- Incorrectly applied monthly account-keeping fees
- ATM withdrawal fees charged in error at Acme-branded ATMs
- Foreign transaction fees applied to transactions made in AUD

## Refund timeframes
- Fee reversals: 3–5 business days
- Disputed card transactions: up to 21 business days while under investigation
- Chargebacks: up to 45 business days, subject to the card scheme rules

## How to request a refund
1. Sign in to the Acme Bank app.
2. Open the transaction and select "Dispute or request refund".
3. Provide a reason and any supporting evidence.

## Non-refundable items
- International ATM operator surcharges
- Third-party merchant fees
- Interest already accrued on a settled balance
