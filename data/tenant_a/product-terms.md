# Acme Bank — Everyday Account Terms

Tenant: Acme Bank (tenant_a)

## Account types
- **Acme Everyday** — transaction account, no minimum balance.
- **Acme Saver** — bonus interest when you deposit $200+ per month and make no withdrawals.

## Interest rates
- Acme Everyday: 0.00% p.a.
- Acme Saver base rate: 0.50% p.a.
- Acme Saver bonus rate: 4.25% p.a. (conditions met)

## Fees
- Monthly account-keeping fee: $0 on Acme Everyday.
- Overseas ATM withdrawal: $5 per withdrawal.
- Replacement card: $10.

## Eligibility
- Must be 14 years or older.
- Australian residency required for online application.
