# Skyline Bank — Skyline Rewards Credit Card

The Skyline Rewards card earns 1 point per $1 spent domestically and 2 points
per $1 spent overseas (foreign currency transactions).

- Annual fee: $129, waived in year 1 for new customers.
- Purchase interest rate: 19.99% p.a.
- Interest-free period: up to 55 days on purchases if the balance is paid in
  full each statement period.
- Points expire 36 months after they are earned.
- Lost or stolen card: customers must call the 24/7 Skyline Bank card
  support line immediately; a replacement card is issued within 5 business
  days.

## Redeeming points

Points can be redeemed for:

- Flight upgrades with Skyline Bank's partner airline (10,000 points = 1
  upgrade voucher).
- Statement credit (100 points = $1 credit, minimum redemption 5,000 points).
- Gift cards from a rotating list of retail partners.
