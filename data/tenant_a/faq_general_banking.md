# Skyline Bank — Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I open a Skyline Saver account?**
Apply through the Skyline Bank mobile app or website with your driver's
licence or passport. Approval is typically instant for amounts under
$10,000; larger initial deposits may require additional identity
verification.

**Can I have more than one Skyline Everyday account?**
Yes, up to three Skyline Everyday accounts per customer, each able to be
given a nickname for budgeting purposes (e.g. "Rent", "Holiday Fund").

**What happens if I lose my phone with the Skyline Bank app installed?**
Log in to internet banking from another device and select "Deregister
device" under Security Settings, or call support to have the app session
revoked remotely. Your account is not at risk from a lost phone alone, as
the app requires biometric or PIN re-authentication.

**How do I increase my daily transfer limit?**
Daily limits can be increased up to $50,000 through internet banking with
two-factor authentication, or in branch with photo ID for higher amounts.

**Does Skyline Bank offer joint accounts?**
Yes, both the Skyline Saver and Skyline Everyday accounts are available
as joint accounts with either "either to sign" or "both to sign"
authority.

**What support channels are available outside business hours?**
The 24/7 phone line handles card loss/theft, fraud reports, and account
lockouts at any hour. General enquiries via the in-app chat are answered
within 1 business day.
