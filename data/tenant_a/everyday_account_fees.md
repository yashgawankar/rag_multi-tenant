# Skyline Bank — Everyday Account Fee Schedule

The Skyline Everyday transaction account is Skyline Bank's primary
day-to-day banking product. The following fees apply unless a fee waiver
condition below is met.

## Standard fees

- Monthly account-keeping fee: $5, waived if at least $2,000 is deposited
  into the account in the calendar month, or if the customer is under 25
  years of age.
- ATM withdrawal at a Skyline Bank ATM: free, unlimited.
- ATM withdrawal at a non-Skyline Bank ATM within Australia: $2.50 per
  withdrawal, plus any fee charged by the ATM operator.
- ATM withdrawal overseas: $5.00 per withdrawal, plus a 3% foreign
  transaction fee on the AUD-converted amount.
- Dishonour fee (insufficient funds for a scheduled payment): $9, capped
  at two dishonour fees per account per month.
- Overdrawn account fee: $9 per day the account remains overdrawn beyond
  an approved limit, capped at $45 per month.
- Paper statement fee: $3 per statement; electronic statements are free.
- Replacement card fee: free for the first replacement in a 12-month
  period, $15 for subsequent replacements.

## Fee waivers

Customers holding a Skyline Everyday account alongside an active Skyline
Variable Home Loan or Skyline Fixed Home Loan (see the home loan product
disclosure document) have the monthly account-keeping fee waived
automatically for the life of the linked home loan, regardless of deposit
amount.

## Overdraft facility

An approved overdraft on a Skyline Everyday account accrues interest at
14.99% p.a., calculated daily on the overdrawn balance and charged
monthly. Overdraft limits range from $500 to $5,000 and are subject to a
separate credit assessment.
