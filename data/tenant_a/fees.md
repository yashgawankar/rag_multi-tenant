# Acme Bank — Fee Schedule

Tenant: Acme Bank (tenant_a)

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Monthly account-keeping (Everyday) | $0 | Always free |
| Overseas ATM withdrawal | $5.00 | Per withdrawal |
| Foreign transaction fee | 3.0% | Of the AUD transaction value |
| Replacement card | $10.00 | Waived for fraud-related replacements |
| Stop payment / cheque | $15.00 | Per request |
| Dishonour fee | $9.00 | When a direct debit cannot be met |

## Fee waivers
- Fraud-related card replacements are always free.
- Customers aged 60+ on an Acme Pension account have all the above fees waived.
